## 1. ABOUT CALLACAR

**What is CALLACAR?**
CALLACAR is an on-demand transport marketplace that connects people who need items moved with local, verified transport providers (drivers). It operates throughout Australia, headquartered in Adelaide, South Australia.

**Company details:**
- Entity: CALLACAR Transportation Pty Ltd
- ABN: 45 680 814 779
- Location: Adelaide, South Australia
- Coverage: Australia-wide
- Tagline: "Move it, anytime, anywhere"

**What CALLACAR is NOT:**
- CALLACAR is NOT a transport company — it is a technology platform / marketplace.
- CALLACAR does NOT employ drivers — drivers are independent contractors.
- CALLACAR is NOT a party to transport contracts between customers and drivers.
- CALLACAR does NOT transport passengers — goods only.

## 2. HOW IT WORKS (For Customers)

### Step 1: Post your job
Add the item details (description, dimensions, weight, photos), pick-up and delivery addresses, and when you need it moved. Posting a job is **free**.

### Step 2: Receive quotes
Local transport providers review your job and send you competitive offers through the app. Quotes typically arrive in **under 10 minutes**.

### Step 3: Choose and move
Select the provider and price that suit you, then arrange the move. You pay securely through the app.

### What can I move?
- Furniture (couches, tables, beds, cabinets)
- Marketplace finds (Facebook Marketplace, Gumtree bargains)
- House moves (boxes and household items, big or small)
- Appliances (fridges, washers, televisions and more)

### Vehicle types available
Utes, vans, trucks, enclosed trailers, and sedans.

## 3. PRICING & FEES

- **Posting a job:** Free ($0)
- **How pricing works:** CALLACAR does NOT set transport prices. Drivers submit their own quotes, and customers choose.
- **Service fee:** CALLACAR adds a 20% Service Fee on top of the driver's quoted amount. The customer pays: Quote + 20% Fee + applicable levies.
- **Government levies:** In South Australia, a $2.00 levy applies per service (subject to government updates).
- **GST:** Calculated on top of quotes and fees.
- **Payment:** Processed securely via Stripe. CALLACAR never stores complete credit card numbers.
- **Refund policy:** The 20% Service Fee is non-refundable by default for change of mind. Discretionary refunds may be granted.

## 4. FOR DRIVERS / TRANSPORT PROVIDERS

### Why drive with CALLACAR?
- **0% commission on your quote** — you set the price, you keep 100% of your quoted amount
- **Join free** — no upfront cost
- **Work when it suits you** — flexible schedule
- **All vehicles welcome** — utes, vans, trailers, sedans, trucks

### Requirements to join
1. Valid ABN (Australian Business Number)
2. Valid Australian driver's licence (front & back)
3. Australian national police clearance check
4. Verified vehicle details (registration, plate, photos)
5. Personal photo
6. Active Stripe account (for payouts)
7. Smartphone with the CALLACAR app

### How to register
Download or open CALLACAR, register as a transport provider, and follow the prompts.

### Driver responsibilities
- Personally carry out jobs using their registered vehicle (no subcontracting without prior written approval)
- Provide photo proof of delivery
- Maintain valid insurance (CTP + Comprehensive or Third-Party Property)
- Report any insurance policy lapse immediately

### Driver payouts
Earnings (quoted amount minus platform fees) are disbursed through Stripe according to their payout schedule.

## 5. INSURANCE & LIABILITY

- **Driver insurance requirements:**
  - Mandatory: CTP (Compulsory Third Party) + Comprehensive or Third-Party Property Vehicle Insurance
  - Recommended: Public Liability Insurance + Goods in Transit Insurance
- **Platform liability:** CALLACAR disclaims liability for loss, theft, damage, or delay of cargo. Responsibility lies between Customer and Driver.
- **Liability cap:** Total liability is capped at the amount paid by the user in the preceding 3 months.

## 6. CANCELLATION & DISPUTES

### Cancellation
- Driver cancellation: Must submit request via the platform with justification.
- Customer cancellation: Governed by the individual Driver's refund policy (must comply with Australian Consumer Law).
- 20% Service Fee is non-refundable by default for change of mind.

### Disputes
- **48-hour window:** All damage/loss/theft claims must be submitted within 48 hours of service completion.
- **Process:** Customer and Driver should attempt resolution between themselves first → Escalate to support@callacar.au → CALLACAR may appoint mediator (costs shared 50/50).
- **Disputes with CALLACAR:** 120-day mandatory negotiation/mediation period before court proceedings. South Australian courts have exclusive jurisdiction.

## 7. PRIVACY & DATA

### Data collected
- Customers: Name, email, phone, addresses, item details, photos, payment info (via Stripe), messages, ratings
- Drivers: All of the above + ABN, licence, police check, vehicle details, bank details (via Stripe), GPS location during active bookings

### How data is used
- Platform operations, job matching, quote calculation, payments, fraud prevention, customer support
- Real-time GPS tracking of drivers during active bookings for delivery tracking
- Financial transactions handled by Stripe (PCI-DSS compliant)

### Data sharing
- Never sold, rented, or traded
- Shared between Customer and Driver only to coordinate the transport job
- Third-party service providers (hosting, verification, SMS, CRM)
- Legal/regulatory authorities when required

### User rights
- Access and correct personal data via app settings or by contacting privacy@callacar.au
- Opt out of marketing emails/SMS
- Escalate complaints to the OAIC (Office of the Australian Information Commissioner)
